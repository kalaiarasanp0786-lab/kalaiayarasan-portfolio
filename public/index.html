Research Projects

Environmental Biotechnology Research
Indoor Algae-Based CO₂ Absorption System Using Chlorella vulgaris

Objective: To develop a sustainable indoor algae cultivation system capable of reducing CO₂ levels while producing algal biomass for environmental applications.

This project investigates the potential of Chlorella vulgaris for indoor CO₂ absorption and biomass production using different nutrient media. The study evaluates algal growth, chlorophyll content, biomass accumulation, and the effectiveness of various culture media under indoor conditions.

📊 Key Results: The natural-source medium demonstrated superior performance, achieving the highest biomass production and chlorophyll content compared to commercial and chemically formulated media — highlighting algae-based systems as cost-effective and sustainable solutions for indoor air quality improvement.
